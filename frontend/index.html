How it works

1

Understands your request

An LLM reads your description and picks up any constraints you mention, such as a date range.

2

Searches by meaning

OpenAlex's semantic search finds papers across 300M+ works that describe your topic, even in different words. The papers those matches cite most often are added too, so the classics aren't missed.

3

Ranks by meaning

A cross-encoder reads every title and abstract against your request. Relevance decides the order, with a small boost for well-cited papers so the classics don't get lost.

Agent mode lets the LLM run the searches itself: it reads abstracts, follows up on promising leads, looks up researchers, and explains why it picked each paper. It's slower but handles multi-part questions and "who works on X?" well.
